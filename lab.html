Our focus

Deep learning for condition monitoring

The Artificial Intelligence for Industrial Applications Lab studies how early and how reliably machine failures can be predicted from sensor data. We develop deep learning models that analyse vibration, current and operational signals from motors, drives, batteries and building systems to support maintenance decisions.

The research is data-driven. Models are developed and validated on public benchmark datasets and on operational data from industrial partners, which keeps results reproducible and directly comparable with published methods.

The lab sits in the Department of AI & Big Data at Woosong University. Students in the lab work on applied diagnosis problems and contribute to peer-reviewed publications.

01 · Methods

Hybrid architectures

Models that combine convolutional, transformer and recurrent components.

02 · Generalisation

Transfer learning

Adapting models trained on one machine to new machines with limited labelled data.

03 · Evidence

Reproducible results

Validation on public benchmarks and partner data, published in peer-reviewed journals.

News

Latest announcements

  1. 2026

    Two student-led papers published

    Shawon Mehadi Hasan published a study of locally runnable large language models for bearing fault diagnosis in Computation. Cinmoy Purkaystha and Yesha Gandhi published uncertainty-aware zero-day botnet detection for IoT networks, deployed on edge hardware, in Sensors.

  2. Ongoing

    Guest editorships: two MDPI special issues

    Prof. Kumar is serving as Guest Editor for the Machines special issue on Prognostics and Health Management for Machinery Safety and Maintenance, and for the Mathematics special issue on Recent Advances in Machine Learning and Deep Learning Theories: Towards Intelligent Fault Diagnosis.

  3. 2025

    Official launch of the AI for Industrial Applications Lab

    The lab is now open in Building W19 on West Campus (Endicott College of International Studies). Its focus is integrating artificial intelligence into manufacturing, logistics and automation, in collaboration with technology firms in Daejeon's Daedeok Innopolis. Students joining the lab get access to GPU computing resources and real industrial datasets from partner organisations.
